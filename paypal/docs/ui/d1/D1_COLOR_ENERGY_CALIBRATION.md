# D1.3a — Color Energy Calibration

Status: **desktop color study for human review**. D1 remains in progress. These values are provisional; D4 owns the final brand-token lock. The accepted C/A/B hybrid layout, product states, fields, and actions are unchanged.

## A–B. Human finding and cause

Human review accepted D1.3 structure and state wayfinding, but found the palette too muted. The old Bone covered most of the canvas with a dusty cast, Sage read as mossy/gray olive, Acid felt pastel, and Vermilion occupied too little of the visible rhythm. The resulting beige → pale Sage → beige sequence had clear hierarchy but insufficient energy. D1.3a changes color and bounded emphasis only.

## C–E. Provisional palette and functional meaning

| Function | D1.3 value | D1.3a exploration value | Meaning retained |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas / neutral work rows | Bone `#F2EDDF` | Cream `#FFF7E8` | Warm editorial reading field, never pure white |
| Text / dividers / CTA | Ink `#161915` | Ink `#17212B` | Structural contrast and primary action |
| Focus / review attention | Vermilion `#EF5B3E` | Vermilion `#F15A3D` | Selective graphic anchor and action-owner emphasis; not generic error |
| Current / pending / waiting | Acid `#EFD94F` | Acid `#F5D12F` | Rail or marker alongside explicit state text |
| Tools / ownership / accepted / resolved | Sage `#8FA47D` | Fresh Mint `#B8DFC4` | Functional zone and supportive state; replaces the old olive feel |
| Finance / evidence reserve | Cobalt `#334EB5` | Cobalt `#3567E8` | Nearly absent in marketplace Set A |

The prototypes use a Cream/Mint local mix for large functional zones and lighter state surfaces. This mix is a study treatment, not an additional global token.

## F. Screen calibration

- **Freelancer Discover:** Cream canvas and normal rows; a clear Fresh Mint filter strip; Acid rail plus Vermilion index for the focused OPEN job; Ink Apply CTA. Other jobs retain neutral rows.
- **Freelancer My Applications:** PENDING has Acid and “Đang chờ Client” without a false action; ACCEPTED has Fresh Mint and “Đã nhận việc” with an Ink assigned-work CTA; REJECTED and CANCELLED stay readable, neutral, and explicit.
- **Client My Jobs:** SUBMITTED_FOR_REVIEW receives the strongest bounded Vermilion attention; REVISION_REQUESTED has an Acid waiting rail; OPEN and IN_PROGRESS stay mostly neutral; COMPLETED gets Fresh Mint; CANCELLED stays quiet.
- **Client Job Detail:** Fresh Mint identifies Client ownership; Acid marks the current OPEN state; a Cream/Mint action zone gets a Vermilion top accent and Ink “Xem ứng tuyển” CTA. Budget remains read-only.

## G–H. Density and anti-patterns

Each screen uses one dominant Cream canvas, one functional Mint surface, one state-specific attention accent, and one strongest Ink action. Vermilion appears as punctuation, focused index, section rule, or review/action accent, not a full-row identity system. Avoid arbitrary row colors, equal-area rainbow blocks, full-screen Acid/Vermilion, neon, poster overload, SaaS cards, glass, glow, and blue Web3 styling. Cobalt is not introduced merely to add variety.

## I. Accessibility and state redundancy

Ink text on the four active light surfaces has calculated contrast ratios of approximately **15.30:1 on Cream**, **11.17:1 on Fresh Mint**, **10.90:1 on Acid**, and **4.86:1 on Vermilion**. Small header role text uses Ink on Vermilion; large Vermilion punctuation is decorative. Historical text is intentionally quieter but retains its written state. Color never acts alone: each application and job state still has its status word plus a rail, ownership sentence, or next-action label. Keyboard focus retains its visible outline.

## J. Review gate

The palette is **not locked**. Compare the D1.3a desktop screenshots at 1440px and 1280px against D1.3 for freshness, focus recognition, calm neutral rows, and editorial restraint. Human review is required before any D1 PASS, D1.5 visual QA completion, D1.6 grammar selection, or D4 token lock.
