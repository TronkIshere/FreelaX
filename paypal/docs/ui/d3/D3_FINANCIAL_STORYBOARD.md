## Failure semantics and hierarchy

The stage rail is a ledger of independent source statuses. A later failure does not undo earlier confirmed evidence. Show the failed stage, last valid evidence, returned error if available, and the actual next possibility. A job-level `TaxExportStatus.FAILED` is different from certificate `EXPORT_FAILED`; a certificate may exist while payout evidence has failed. Absence of a payout or tax record is not a zero balance or failed certificate.

The statement surface puts human outcome and stage labels first. Amounts, fee, masked bank, rate source, network, and timestamps form supporting rows. Signatures, PDAs, public keys, invoice/withdrawal IDs, and real DEVNET Explorer URLs (only if returned) go under event-specific **Technical proof**. Do not construct Explorer links from the fixture placeholders. The [evidence hierarchy](D3_EVIDENCE_HIERARCHY.md) and [honesty rules](D3_SIMULATION_EVIDENCE_RULES.md) govern every frame.
