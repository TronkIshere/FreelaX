## 2. Actors and roles
| Actor | On-chain identity | May | May not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buyer (client) | `msg.sender` equal to the escrow's client | approve and `fund`, `approveExtension`, `requestRevision`, `openDispute`, `release`, mutual refund | act for others |
| Freelancer | `msg.sender` equal to the escrow's freelancer | `requestExtension`, `submit`, `openDispute`, mutual refund | release early |
| Backend authorization signer | `AUTH_SIGNER_ROLE` key (ephemeral dev signer) | sign EIP-712 authorizations for `fund`, `submit`, `requestRevision` | submit fund-moving transactions, resolve disputes, pause, change roles |
| Arbiter | per-escrow address (see section 4) | `resolveDispute` on a Disputed escrow | release/refund non-disputed escrows; sign authorizations |
| Relayer | no role | call permissionless `release` after the deadline, pay gas | anything privileged; the contract must not require it |
| Pauser | `PAUSER_ROLE` | `pause` (new funding only) | unpause, move funds, block release or refund |
| Administrator | `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` (multisig is **not** required in the simulation; a development key under the same signer rules) | grant/revoke roles, rotate the authorization signer, set the default arbiter for **new** escrows, unpause | withdraw escrowed funds, override an escrow state, change an existing escrow's arbiter implicitly |
Negative tests for every "may not" cell are acceptance criteria (Phase 1 lab tests already show the pattern: signer cannot use operator powers, operator cannot grant roles, rotation revokes the old key).

## 3. Operations (mapping preserved; see 03 section 7 for preconditions)
`fund`, `requestExtension`, `approveExtension`, `submit`, `requestRevision`, `openDispute`, `release` (permissionless once eligible), `resolveDispute`, `mutualRefund`, `pause`/`unpause`, role management. Timing boundaries are copied verbatim and tested on both sides: submit `now <= deliveryDueAt`; dispute from Funded `now > deliveryDueAt`; revision and dispute from Submitted `now < reviewDueAt`; release by a non-client `now >= reviewDueAt`; no unilateral refund exists. Bounded parameters: review window 24 to 168 hours, one extension of at most 7 days, `maxRevisions <= 2`, high-value grace (+24 h above 500 USDC when requested). The 72 h window and `maxRevisions = 2` stay **parameters bound by the backend authorization**, not constants (Phase 0 clarification 1). Fees stay off-chain; full amount in, full amount out. `block.timestamp` is second-resolution and is the including block's header time (Phase 1: not "now"); deadlines near the boundary can fail for a transaction submitted earlier; the backend re-reads escrow state before treating a boundary revert as a business failure.

## 4. Arbiter semantics (evaluated, not decided)
| Option | Behaviour | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Per-escrow snapshot (Solana parity) | the arbiter address is fixed in the escrow record at `fund`; a new default arbiter set later by the admin applies **only to escrows funded afterwards** | exact parity with `config.admin` snapshotted at fund; no implicit permission change on existing escrows | a lost arbiter key strands that escrow's dispute |
| B. Global role | any holder of `ARBITER_ROLE` may resolve any Disputed escrow; admin rotates the role | simple recovery | **implicit global rotation changes permissions of existing escrows**: explicitly excluded by the owner's direction |
| C. Snapshot plus explicit per-escrow reassignment | A, plus an admin action `reassignArbiter(escrowId, newArbiter)` that emits an event, is limited to Disputed or Funded escrows, and may require both parties' acceptance or a delay | recovery without silent permission change | a new rule not present in Solana: needs owner approval |
**Plan:** implement **A** in P2-M5/M6. C is a decision (O-3) and is **not** built unless approved. The administrator's "default arbiter" setter affects new escrows only and is covered by a test that proves existing escrows keep their arbiter.

## 5. Isolation, custody and the USDC freeze case (design gate)
D2 baseline is a **shared immutable contract with logically isolated per-escrow records**. Solana has per-escrow vault custody; a shared contract does not (see 03 section 10). The shared design is **not assumed safe for every USDC blacklist and custody case**: if Circle's blacklister froze the contract address, all open escrows would freeze; a clone per escrow would limit this to one. **Gate (P2-M5 exit, owner decision O-2):** produce a short comparison (shared vs EIP-1167 factory/clones: blacklist blast radius, per-escrow verifiability, audit surface, deployment count, operational monitoring) with **measured** gas only; no unmeasured gas claims. Until the gate is decided, M5 may start with the shared baseline behind an interface that does not preclude clones (`escrowId` addressing kept in events).
Payout under a USDC blacklist of the **recipient**: open question 9. Options to evaluate on a Fuji fork with impersonated issuer roles (the only way to test it; Fuji roles are not available): (i) push payout in `release` (reverts if the recipient is frozen, leaving the escrow Submitted/Disputed), (ii) pull-payout into a claimable balance, (iii) pull-payout plus an admin reroute (rejected by the role rule that no role may reach escrowed funds). No option is chosen here.

## 6. EIP-712 authorizations
Domain: `name`, `version`, `chainId`, `verifyingContract`. Typed structs (placeholders): `FundAuthorization` (client, freelancer, token, amount, `fundingExpiresAt`, `deliveryDueAt`, `reviewWindowHours`, `maxRevisions`, `highValueGrace`, arbiter, escrowId, nonce, deadline), `SubmitAuthorization` (escrowId, `evidenceHash`, `submissionCount`, nonce, deadline), `RevisionAuthorization` (escrowId, `feedbackHash`, `revisionsUsed`, nonce, deadline; necessity open, question 2), `MutualRefund` signed by both parties (escrowId, nonce, deadline). Per-escrow nonces and short deadlines; the contract recovers with `ECDSA` (rejects high-s) and compares with the **current** authorization signer; rotation affects only new authorizations. The backend produces a signature only from **MySQL-recorded terms** (72 h and `maxRevisions` checked against the ledger); never for unrecorded evidence. Phase 1 evidence: Java digest equals the contract digest, web3j equals Foundry `cast`, low-s is mandatory (a high-s signature reverts with `ECDSAInvalidSignatureS`).
EIP-712 typed-data signing, EIP-2612 permit and EIP-3009 `transferWithAuthorization` are **different mechanisms** and are not interchangeable. MVP funding uses `approve` then `fund` (verified on a Fuji fork); permit/EIP-3009 are optional later optimisations (both verified behaviourally on Fuji USDC in Phase 1, not on a Mainnet token: out of scope).
